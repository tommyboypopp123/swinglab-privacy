SwingLab — Privacy Policy

Effective August 15, 2026

The short version

SwingLab does not collect your data. It has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, and no tracking of any kind. The app contains no networking code at all — your videos and measurements physically cannot leave your iPhone unless you choose to share them yourself.

What SwingLab records

  • Video and audio of your swing, when you tap Record. Audio is recorded as part of the video and is used to pinpoint the moment of ball contact from the sound of the strike.
  • Depth and distance measurements from your iPhone's LiDAR sensor during calibration, used to convert on-screen distances into real inches.
  • Videos you choose to import from your photo library. SwingLab only ever receives the specific videos you pick; it has no access to the rest of your library.
  • Details you enter yourself: your height, handedness, and club selection.
  • Measurements calculated from your swings, and any names or drawings you add.

Where it is stored

All of it stays in SwingLab's private storage on your iPhone. Nothing is uploaded, backed up to us, or shared with anyone. We never see it, because there is no “we” on the receiving end — there is no server.

What SwingLab never does

  • No account or sign-in, ever.
  • No analytics, telemetry, crash reporting, or usage tracking.
  • No advertising, and no data sold or shared with advertisers or data brokers.
  • No third-party code or SDKs of any kind.
  • No access to your contacts, location, health data, or anything else beyond the camera, microphone, and the videos you pick.

When data does leave your phone

Only when you deliberately send it. If you use the share button on a swing report, a marked-up frame, or the CSV export, iOS hands that file to whatever app or person you choose. From that point it is governed by wherever you sent it — a text message, an email, Photos, and so on. SwingLab is not involved in that transfer and keeps no copy of where it went.

Deleting your data

Swipe to delete any swing or video in the app and its recording, measurements, calibration, and drawings are erased with it. Deleting SwingLab from your iPhone removes everything at once. Because nothing was ever sent anywhere, there is nothing else to request or erase.

Other people in your videos

If you film someone else — a playing partner, a student, a stranger at the range — that footage is yours to handle responsibly. Please get their okay before recording or sharing video of them. The app has no way to know who is in frame, so this part is on you.

Children

SwingLab is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, of any age.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the updated version will appear here and in the app, with a new effective date. Since the app collects nothing, any change would be a clarification rather than a new use of your data.
